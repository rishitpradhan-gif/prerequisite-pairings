# Related work and priority

- [Poset Positional Games, FUN 2024](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.FUN.2024.2): the prerequisite-poset game framework predates this project.
- [Combinatorial Games: Tic-Tac-Toe Theory](https://www.cs.umd.edu/~gasarch/COURSES/752/S22/Combgamesttt.pdf): classical pairing-strategy background.
- [Allis, A Knowledge-based Approach of Connect-Four (1988)](https://tromp.github.io/c4/connect4_thesis.pdf): established Connect Four strategy background.
- [Earlier project](https://github.com/rishitpradhan-gif/connect4-proof-lab): fixed-response gravity-chain classification and the earlier 16-ply obstruction on width nine.

A targeted search examined prerequisite-poset pairing strategies and fixed-response Connect Four obstructions. It did not establish priority for these precise statements. We make no “first ever,” unpublished-literature, or global novelty claim. The mathematical contribution offered here is the exact theorem and its reproducible evidence, with its hypotheses stated explicitly.
